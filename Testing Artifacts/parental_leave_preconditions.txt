- The applicant must be a working parent or a person exercising parental responsibility, including a biological, adoptive or foster parent, or a presumed mother under Article 1464 of the Civil Code who has a child through surrogacy.

- The child must be under eight (8) years of age.

- The applicant must be employed either under a dependent-employment relationship or under a paid mandate, in the private or public sector. In the public sector, the subsidy is available only to employees covered by labour legislation.

- The applicant must have completed one (1) year of employment with the same employer, whether continuously or through successive fixed-term employment contracts, unless more favourable terms are provided by specific legislation, decrees, regulations, collective labour agreements, arbitration awards, or oral or written employer–employee agreements.

- The employer must upload the parental-leave grant form (Form E.14) to the ERGANI Information System.

- For online submission, the applicant must hold TAXISnet credentials to access DYPA electronic services through certification by the Independent Authority for Public Revenue (AADE).

- The applicant must be registered in DYPA’s Register of Natural Persons and hold a DYPA Registration Number; where not already registered, registration is completed electronically using TAXISnet credentials, following verification by AADE.
