- The applicant must be a working parent or a person exercising parental responsibility, including a biological parent, adoptive parent, foster parent, or a presumed mother under Article 1464 of the Civil Code who has a child through surrogacy.

- The child must be under eight (8) years of age.

- The applicant must be employed either under a dependent-employment relationship or under a paid mandate, in the private or public sector. In the public sector, only employees subject to labour legislation are eligible for the benefit from the Public Employment Service (DYPA).

- The applicant must have completed one (1) year of employment with the same employer, either continuously or under successive fixed-term employment contracts, unless more favourable terms are provided by specific legislation, decrees, regulations, collective labour agreements, arbitration awards, or oral or written agreements between employers and employees.

- The employer must have uploaded the Parental Leave Grant Form (Form E.14) to the ERGANI Information System.

- For electronic submission, the applicant must hold TAXISnet credentials to log in to DYPA’s electronic services through certification by the Independent Authority for Public Revenue (AADE).

- The applicant must be registered in DYPA’s Register of Natural Persons and hold a DYPA Registration Number; if not already registered, registration must be completed electronically using TAXISnet credentials, with personal details verified by AADE.
