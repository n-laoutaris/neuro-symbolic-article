- The applicant must be a working parent or a person exercising parental responsibility, including a biological, adoptive, foster, or presumed mother under Article 1464 of the Civil Code who has a child through surrogacy.

- The child must not have reached eight (8) years of age.

- The applicant must be employed either under a dependent-employment relationship or under a paid mandate, in the private or public sector. In the public sector, only employees governed by labour legislation are eligible for the DYPA benefit.

- The applicant must have completed one (1) year of employment with the same employer, either continuously or through successive fixed-term employment contracts, unless more favourable terms are provided by specific legislation, decrees, regulations, collective labour agreements, arbitration awards, or oral or written employer–employee agreements.

- The employer must upload the parental-leave grant declaration (Form E.14) to the ERGANI Information System.

- If not already registered, the insured person must register in DYPA’s Register of Natural Persons and obtain a DYPA Registration Number.

- For electronic submission, the applicant must hold TAXISnet credentials to access DYPA’s electronic services through certification by the Independent Authority for Public Revenue (AADE).
