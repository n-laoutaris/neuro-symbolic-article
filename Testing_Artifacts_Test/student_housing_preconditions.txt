- The student must be a Greek national or a national of another European Union Member State.
- The student must hold a valid academic identity card.
- The student must live, for study purposes, in rented accommodation in a city other than that of their principal residence.
  - Neither the student nor their parents may hold full ownership or usufruct rights over a residence in the city where the student rents accommodation.
  - The lease must be valid for at least six months.
- The annual family income for the preceding year must not exceed **€30,000**, increased by **€3,000 for each dependent child beyond the first**.
- The student’s parents, or the student themself, must not own or hold usufruct rights over residences—whether owner-occupied or rented out—exceeding a total of **200 sq. m.**
  - Residences or apartments located in a municipality or community with fewer than 3,000 inhabitants are excluded from this calculation.
- The student must have successfully passed examinations in at least half of the courses of the preceding academic year for which the allowance is requested.
- Where a parent is the beneficiary:
  - The student’s and the supporting parent’s Tax Identification Numbers (AFM) must be declared.
  - If the parents are divorced or legally separated, the beneficiary is the parent who supports the student.
  - The supporting parent must have submitted an income tax return for the year in which the housing-allowance application is made.
- The student may exceptionally be the beneficiary if they:
  - are orphaned of both parents; or
  - have parents residing abroad; or
  - are over 25 years old; or
  - are required to submit an income tax return and are not considered a dependent member under Article 11 of Law 4172/2013.
  - In this case, only the spouse’s AFM, if applicable, is declared.
- To qualify for the enhanced allowance due to cohabitation, the cohabiting person must:
  - be another undergraduate student at a higher education institution pursuing their first degree;
  - not have exceeded the prescribed period of study, in semesters, required to obtain the degree under the programme of study;
  - not already hold another higher-education degree;
  - study in the same city or regional unit as the student for whom the allowance application is submitted;
  - be named as the tenant, or be included as a dependent child in the tax return of the person named as tenant, under the AFM declared for the cohabiting student; and
  - be included as a tenant in the lease submitted with the housing-allowance application.
